Installation

Mount it in an afternoon.

You will need a drill, a level, a pencil, and the right anchors for your wall. Take your time with the marking.

  1. 01

    Pick your spot

    Sit in your chair first. Mark the height where your knees clear the open desk. Choose a wall with solid fixing points, not a thin partition on its own.

  2. 02

    Mark and level

    Hold the brackets up, mark the holes with a pencil, and check with a level. A straight line now means a steady desk later.

  3. 03

    Drill and fit anchors

    Drill the holes and fit anchors that match your wall. Use the table on the right. If you are unsure, ask the hardware store to see your wall type.

  4. 04

    Fix the brackets

    Screw the brackets firmly into the anchors. Hang the unit and give it a firm pull. It should not move.

  5. 05

    Fold and test

    Fold the desk down and place a laptop and notebooks on it. Sit and work for a few minutes. Adjust the height if needed.

Tools

Drill with masonry bit · Level · Pencil · Tape measure · Screwdriver · Hammer for anchors

Wall typeFixingNote

Safety notes

Never mount on a thin partition without backing. Keep the load to 15 kg evenly distributed. If the wall feels hollow or crumbly, stop and get advice before drilling.

How it is made

Eight steps from board to box.

Done slowly, in a small workshop in Candaba. The slow steps are the ones you cannot see.

FIG. 01 — The workshop bench

Made in small batches

We make 12 to 15 units a month. Every unit is checked by hand before it goes into the box. Offcuts go back into the workshop for jigs and small parts.

  1. 01

    Select the wood panel

    Choose a palochina panel with even grain and no cracks.

  2. 02

    Measure and mark

    Mark every cut line twice. Measure twice, cut once.

  3. 03

    Cut the parts

    Cut the shelf board, side supports and brackets to size.

  4. 04

    Sand and smooth

    Sand every surface until it feels smooth to the hand. This takes longer than cutting.

  5. 05

    Assemble

    Join the parts with screws and wood glue. Check alignment as you go.

  6. 06

    Inspect

    Check the structure and alignment. Fold it open and closed. Fix anything that wobbles.

  7. 07

    Apply the finish

    Apply the natural varnish in the light oak tone, evenly.

  8. 08

    Dry, test and pack

    Let it dry and cure. Fit the hinges, run a quality test, then pack it in the kraft box with bubble wrap.

About

Our story

EverWood Designs began as a feasibility study. Four marketing students at Pampanga State University, Candaba Campus, kept seeing the same problem: classmates and boarders trying to study in rooms too small for a desk.

So they researched it. They surveyed 383 people. Everyone had heard of convertible furniture, most were interested, almost none owned one. Quality and durability mattered most, then price. That became the brief: a desk that folds away, made of wood, at ₱499.

Today the FlexiWall Desk is made in small batches, 12 to 15 units a month, in a workshop in Pansol, Pasig, Candaba, Pampanga. “Ever” stands for longevity and reliability. “Wood” stands for warmth and craftsmanship. “Designs” stands for thoughtful, modern, functional.

Our vision is to be a leading innovator in space-saving furniture in the Philippines. Our mission is simple: durable, innovative furniture that supports sustainable living while maximizing comfort and efficiency.
